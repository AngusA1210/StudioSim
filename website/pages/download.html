Download · Version {{version}}

Free for macOS and Windows

Everything StudioSim needs comes with it: there is nothing else to install. Earlier versions, and what changed in each, are on the releases page.

macOS

A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) · macOS 12 or later

Download for Mac
  1. Open StudioSim-macOS.dmg and drag StudioSim onto Applications.
  2. Open StudioSim from Applications. The first time, macOS says it can't check it: click Done.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway beside StudioSim and confirm. From then on it opens as any app does.

VST3 and Audio Unit plug-ins, any Core Audio interface and MIDI device. The audio bridge for desktop audio needs macOS 14.2.

Windows

A 64-bit PC · Windows 10 or 11

Download for Windows
  1. Run StudioSim-Windows-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click through the installer. It installs for you alone and asks for no administrator's password, then opens StudioSim. It's in the Start menu from then on, and uninstalls from Settings › Apps.

VST3 plug-ins and MIDI devices. This version plays through Windows' own audio (WASAPI), which works with every interface; ASIO comes in a later version.

What you'll want

Wired headphones

Every sound is placed around you, which needs headphones. Bluetooth ones add a delay of 150 to 250 ms that no software can remove, so use a cable.

An audio interface, if you play

Plug a guitar, a bass or a microphone into your interface and it plays through an amp or a speaker in the room. Not needed for the built-in instruments.

A MIDI keyboard, if you have one

It plays any instrument whose input is on, with velocity. The computer keyboard plays them too.

Why the warnings

Apple and Microsoft warn about any app that isn't signed with a certificate bought from them. StudioSim isn't, yet: the step above is needed once, the first time. Each release's checksums are on the releases page, to check a download against.

New to it?

Start with Tutorial on the title screen: it takes you through a room of your own a step at a time. Then the guide has the rest.
