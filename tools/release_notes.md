StudioSim {{version}}: a room of instruments, heard from where you stand.

## Download

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later): `StudioSim-macOS.dmg`. Open it and drag StudioSim to Applications.
- **Windows** (64-bit, Windows 10 or 11): `StudioSim-Windows-Setup.exe`. Run it; it installs for you alone and asks
  for no administrator's password.

## The first time you open it

StudioSim isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so each warns about it once:

- **macOS**: open StudioSim, and when macOS says it can't check it, click **Done**. Then open **System Settings ›
  Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway** next to StudioSim, and confirm. After that it opens
  normally.
- **Windows**: when SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Use **wired** headphones: everything you hear is placed around you, and Bluetooth adds a delay no software can remove.
New to it? **Tutorial**, on the title screen, takes you through a room of your own.

On Windows this release uses Windows' own audio (WASAPI), which works with every interface; ASIO comes in a later
release.
